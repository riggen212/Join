Help

Welcome to the help page for Join, your guide to using our kanban project management tool. Here, we'll provide an overview of what Join is, how it can benefit you, and how to use it.

What is Join?

Join is a kanban-based project management tool designed and built by a group of dedicated students as part of their web development bootcamp at the Developer Akademie GmbH.

Kanban, a Japanese term meaning "billboard", is a highly effective method to visualize work, limit work-in-progress, and maximize efficiency (or flow). Join leverages the principles of kanban to help users manage their tasks and projects in an intuitive, visual interface.

It is important to note that Join is designed as an educational exercise and is not intended for extensive business usage. While we strive to ensure the best possible user experience, we cannot guarantee consistent availability, reliability, accuracy, or other aspects of quality regarding Join.

How to use it

Here is a step-by-step guide on how to use Join:

  1. 1. Getting an overview

    After logging in to Join, the "Summary" page shows your key metrics at a glance: how many tasks are in each column, how many tasks are urgent and when the next deadline is due.

  2. 2. Exploring the Board

    The "Board" represents your project and contains four columns: "To do", "In progress", "Await feedback" and "Done". Each card on the board is a task. Use the "Find Task" field to search tasks by title or description.

  3. 3. Creating Contacts

    Go to "Contacts", click on "Add new contact" and enter a name, email and phone number. Once added, contacts can be assigned to tasks.

  4. 4. Adding Tasks

    Open "Add Task" in the navigation or click "Add Task" on the board. With the "+" button next to a column heading, the new task is created directly in that column. Enter a title, a due date and a category (required), and optionally a description, a priority (Urgent, Medium, Low), assigned contacts and subtasks.

  5. 5. Viewing and Editing Tasks

    Click a card to open its details. There you can check off subtasks, edit the task or delete it.

  6. 6. Moving Tasks

    As a task moves from one stage to another, drag and drop its card into another column. With the keyboard, select a card, press Space to start moving it, choose a column with the left and right arrow keys and press Enter to confirm or Escape to cancel.

  7. 7. Deleting Tasks

    Once a task is completed, you can either move it to "Done" or delete it in the task details. Deleting a task permanently removes it from the board. Please be careful, as this action cannot be undone.

Remember that using Join effectively requires consistent updates from you and your team to ensure the board reflects the current state of your project.
