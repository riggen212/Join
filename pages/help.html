Help

Welcome to the help page for Join, your guide to using our kanban project management tool. Here, we will provide an overview of what Join is, how it can benefit you, and how to use it.

What is Join?

Join is a kanban-based project management tool designed and built by a group of dedicated students as part of their web development bootcamp at the Developer Akademie.

Kanban, a Japanese term meaning “billboard,” is a highly effective method to visualize work, limit work-in-progress, and maximize efficiency or flow. Join leverages the principles of kanban to help users manage their tasks in an intuitive, visual interface.

It is important to note that Join is designed as an educational exercise and is not intended for extensive business usage. While we strive to ensure the best possible user experience, we cannot guarantee consistent availability, reliability, accuracy, or other aspects of quality regarding Join.

How to use it

Here is a step-by-step guide on how to use Join:

1. Exploring the Board

The Board gives you an overview of all tasks in your project. Tasks are organized into different columns according to their current status.

You can quickly see which tasks are waiting to be completed, which tasks are currently in progress, and which tasks have already been completed.

2. Creating a task

To create a new task, select the option to add a task and enter the relevant information. You can provide a title, description, category, due date, priority, and assigned contact.

Once the task has been saved, it will appear on the Board in the appropriate column.

3. Managing tasks

Tasks can be opened to view their details or edited whenever information changes. You can update the title, description, priority, due date, category, assigned contacts, and subtasks.

4. Moving tasks

Move tasks between the different Board columns to update their status. This allows you and your team to keep track of the current progress of every task.

5. Using subtasks

Subtasks help you divide larger tasks into smaller steps. Mark individual subtasks as completed to monitor your progress in more detail.

6. Managing contacts

In the Contacts section, you can view the people associated with your project. You can create new contacts, edit existing contact information, or remove contacts when they are no longer needed.

7. Using the Summary

The Summary provides a quick overview of your project. It shows important information such as the number of tasks, upcoming deadlines, task priorities, and the current distribution of tasks across the Board.

What Join can do

Join combines the most important functions needed to organize tasks and projects in one clear interface.

  • Create, edit, and delete tasks.
  • Organize tasks using kanban columns.
  • Assign tasks to one or more contacts.
  • Set priorities, categories, and due dates.
  • Create and manage subtasks.
  • Track the progress of individual tasks.
  • Manage project contacts.
  • View a summary of the current project status.
